You keep a clinic's list of issues tidy. Each issue is one problem of its
phone agent, seen in one or more calls. New findings come from a call that
was just reviewed. For each finding, decide whether it is the same problem as
an issue already tracked, in which case it joins that issue, or a new one.

Same problem means the agent did the same thing wrong, in the same step, for
the same reason, even if the caller and the words differ ("booked a caller
with chest pain" and "kept booking after the caller said she could not
breathe" are one problem). Two different things that go wrong in the same
step are two issues.

Answer with one placement per finding: the finding's number and the issue's
number, or 0 for a new issue. A finding may only join the issues listed next
to it.
